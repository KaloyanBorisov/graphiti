ACME Corporation Employee Handbook
Effective January 1, 2024

Section 1: Remote Work Policy

ACME Corporation supports flexible remote work arrangements for eligible
employees. Employees who wish to work remotely must submit a request to
their manager at least two weeks in advance. Remote work is evaluated on
a role-by-role basis, and approval is not guaranteed.

Employees working remotely are expected to maintain the same availability
and responsiveness as they would in the office, generally between 9:00 AM
and 5:00 PM in their local time zone. All remote employees must have a
reliable internet connection and a private space suitable for video calls.

Section 2: Time Off and Leave Policies

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar
year, accrued monthly. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days
to the following year. Any PTO beyond that cap is forfeited unless local
law requires otherwise.

In addition to PTO, ACME observes 10 paid holidays annually. Employees
requiring extended leave, such as parental or medical leave, should
contact Human Resources to discuss eligibility under applicable federal
and state law.

Section 3: Professional Development

ACME reimburses up to $1,500 per year per employee for approved courses,
certifications, and conference attendance. Requests must be submitted
through the Professional Development portal and approved by a manager
before the expense is incurred.

Employees are also encouraged to use the internal mentorship program,
which pairs new hires with senior staff for their first six months.

Section 4: Performance Management

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in June and December.
Reviews are based on goals set collaboratively between the employee and
their manager at the start of each review period. Compensation changes,
when applicable, are typically effective the pay period following the
December review.

Section 5: Workplace Conduct

ACME is committed to maintaining a respectful and inclusive workplace.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are strictly
prohibited. Employees who experience or witness misconduct are encouraged
to report it to their manager or directly to Human Resources.

Section 6: Information Security

Employees must use company-issued devices for accessing internal systems
whenever possible. Multi-factor authentication is required for all
company accounts. Any suspected security incident, such as a phishing
email or lost device, must be reported to the IT Security team within
24 hours.

Section 7: Benefits

ACME offers medical, dental, and vision insurance, effective the first
day of the month following an employee's start date. The company matches
401(k) contributions up to 4% of an employee's salary.
